# Third-Party Notices

## Papercut homepage (2026-11-05)

The new homepage adapts textures, halftone and torn-paper geometry, stamp
composition, and animation timing from OpenSlide's Papercut Zine Fest demo.
Source: https://github.com/open-slide/open-slide (commit
`4f09a4e4875a0be7fb1083932709073af6377092`),
`apps/demo/slides/papercut-zine-fest/index.tsx`.
MIT copyright 2026 Yiwei Ho; full notice: `assets/papercut/OPEN-SLIDE-LICENSE.txt`.
The source has been adapted to native CSS/JavaScript and Jekyll.
`assets/js/papercut-primitives.js` preserves the upstream `rng`, `torn`,
`tapeClip`, `dots` and `dotsDiag` algorithms. The matching primitive stylesheet
adapts RisoText, Scrap, Tape, Badge, RectStamp and RoundStamp to responsive
HTML. See `assets/papercut/upstream-manifest.json` for component mappings.
The occasional hero heartbeat is a site-specific extension, not an upstream loop.

Bricolage Grotesque, Courier Prime and Rubik Mono One fonts are self-hosted
WOFF2 files from Google Fonts (`ofl/bricolagegrotesque`, `ofl/courierprime`,
`ofl/rubikmonoone`).
Their SIL Open Font License notices are included under `assets/papercut/`.
Institution logos and project media retain their existing sources and ownership.
